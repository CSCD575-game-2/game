Welcome Commander!

**********************

The Galaxy needs you to save it!
Sadly, the only men we had on hand are fresh out of training, they'll need to learn on the fly through your commands!

There are 4 dimensions to commanding your troops:
- 1: <color=#00FF00>RECON</color> vs <color=#00FF00>ENGAGE</color>; Recon tells them to focus on discovering the enemy ship, engage tells them to worry about attacking enemy ships.

- 2: <color=#00FF00>DEFENSE</color> vs <color=#00FF00>ASSAULT</color>; tells them to focus more on either defending our mothership, or trying to destroy the enemy's mothership instead.

-3: <color=#00FF00>TACTICS</color> vs <color=#00FF00>STRATEGY</color>; this one tells the troops to other look at the long game, and consider all the combat they've seen up to that point, or, focus on adapting immediately to the enemies tactics. This one might take some getting used to.

- 4: <color=#00FF00>DOCTRINE</color> vs <color=#00FF00>CHANGE</color>: This is for telling the troops how much they need to either focus on staying alive, or trying to follow your orders. I know it's weird to tell your soldiers to ignore your orders, but we can't win a war with a bunch of dead men.

You can customize your command doctrine or choose from one of 4 <color=#00FF00>PRESET</color> options. Try to find the one that is the most effective!

**********************

Speaking of which, try to keep your pilots alive commander. They may be dumber than a box of rocks at the start, but these boys learn quick. The longer they fight, the smarter they get!

For now we only can supply you with 2 types of ships, <color=#00FF00>FIGHTERS</color> and <color=#00FF00>RESUPPLY</color>. You already know what the fighters do, but the resupply ships keep your fighters going. Without them, that little yellow bar beneath the fighter's health will go empty, leaving that ship stranded in the middle of space. I shouldn't have to tell you why that's bad.

You can give your ships target priorities by using the <color=#00FF00>PRIORITY</color> command.

Keep your eye on the enemy and your own ships commander, it may look like your troops aren't obeying your orders, but they're just trying to figure it out, give them a bit of time. Well, that should be just about everything.

Oh, and one more thing before we let you go, if you are under direct attack at your mothership, use the DEFEND action to fire onenemy ships.

**********************

Good luck out there commander, and God's speed!
